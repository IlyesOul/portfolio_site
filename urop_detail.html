MIT-Harvard Biomedical Center · Dec 2025–Mar 2026

deep learning for medical imaging

undergraduate research at the intersection of computer vision and cardiology.

research focus

i developed Python/PyTorch workflows for automated coronary plaque image segmentation, comparing optical coherence tomography (OCT) and optical attenuation coefficient (OAC) imaging.

working with clinical mentors, i connected quantitative model comparisons to the imaging questions behind the project.

architecture benchmarking

i systematically benchmarked 14 neural network architectures, including CNNs, U-Nets, and transformer-based models, using automated training pipelines for reproducible comparisons.

model optimization

i automated hyperparameter sweeps and cross-validation experiments to address noisy labels and severe class imbalance. the resulting improvements increased mean IoU by approximately 6.5 percentage points.

lessons for healthcare ML

the project made data quality and evaluation central to how i approach ML. medical images can have ambiguous boundaries and uneven class distributions; a useful comparison needs to account for both.

key research areas

  • coronary plaque segmentation
  • OCT / OAC modality comparison
  • U-Net optimization & parameter sweeps
  • cross-validation under class imbalance
  • clinical interpretation of findings

tools & technologies

Python, PyTorch, neural segmentation architectures, automated parameter sweeps, and cross-validated model evaluation.
