independent studies · 2023/2024 & 2024/2025

learning ML by building

a curriculum i designed, proposed to my computer science teacher, and explored through hands-on projects.

core ML techniques

the first study focused on supervised and unsupervised learning through projects such as a prioritized to-do list and stock clustering. i then explored optimization and neural networks in Keras, including an LSTM for stock prediction.

from models to pipelines

the second study extended that foundation into preprocessing, feature engineering, boosting, and more advanced neural architectures, with an emphasis on building end-to-end ML pipelines.

the curriculum

  • supervised learning
  • unsupervised learning
  • model evaluation, optimization & preprocessing
  • neural networks
  • experimental applications
  • advanced neural network techniques
  • ML pipelines
  • natural language processing

putting ideas into practice

new topics became new projects or additions to existing ones, including Theta Terminal, Intellivest, and the prioritized to-do list. the studies established an approach i still use: learn the concept, implement it, and examine what happens on data.

where it began

during my sophomore year of high school, i took two college-level data structures and algorithms courses at Bunker Hill Community College, covering binary trees, linked lists, and hashing. wanting to apply those ideas helped motivate the independent studies.

where it led

the studies became a foundation for my later MIT coursework, biomedical imaging research, Fidelity internship, and current work in real-world computer vision.
