why I create

curiosity into practice

i’m drawn to questions that require both careful experiments and something tangible at the end. my recent work spans coronary plaque segmentation, financial document analysis, and computer vision for coastal monitoring.

i’m especially interested in robotic perception and autonomy, and in healthcare applications where better models can support better decisions.

how I work

i enjoy owning a problem from data preparation to evaluation: building baselines, testing assumptions, comparing models, and understanding where they fail.

collaboration is central to that process. working with researchers, clinicians, and analysts has taught me to connect technical results to the questions that actually matter to the people using them.
