ML for financial due diligence

Data Strategy Intern — document analysis, retrieval, and statistical risk modeling.

the problem

financial due diligence involves reviewing large amounts of institutional documentation. as a Data Strategy Intern at Fidelity Investments Strategic Advisors, i worked on tools to help analysts extract information and surface relevant signals more efficiently.

what I built

i developed an internal due-diligence analytics application using Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. the application automated document-analysis workflows and reduced manual analyst review time by approximately 50%.

retrieval & document processing

i engineered document-processing and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines to extract structured data from institutional questionnaires. vector indexing and dense embedding search supplied relevant domain context for queries over financial documents.

sentiment & risk signals

i integrated a fine-tuned FinBERT model for sentiment and risk analysis, alongside statistical anomaly-detection methods including Isolation Forests, Gaussian copulas, and kernel density estimation.

these methods became components of modular risk-analysis workflows, with detection thresholds evaluated through cross-validation.

what I took away

this internship connected model development with software that people could use in a daily workflow. it reinforced my interest in practical ML: making information accessible, evaluating the signals carefully, and building the surrounding application.

tools & technologies

  • Python · JavaScript · HTML/CSS
  • FinBERT · NLP
  • RAG · vector indexing · dense embeddings
  • Isolation Forests · Gaussian copulas · KDE
  • cross-validation · modular application development
